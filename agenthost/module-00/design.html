Start with the operating model

The hosting decision is a balance of seven forces.

01 Isolation

Managed per-agent, container, or micro-VM boundaries.

02 Lifecycle

Warm response, suspend/resume, hibernation, and scale-to-zero.

03 Control

Managed defaults versus ownership of runtime, network, and nodes.

04 Stateful

Durable conversation and tool context across restart and eviction.

05 Identity & Auth

Entra ID, managed identities, token validation, and workload identity.

06 Governance

Audit, policy, tenant boundaries, networking, compliance, and quotas.

07 Economics

Idle cost, cold start, capacity model, and operational overhead.

02 / 12

Two audiences, different priorities

Enterprise optimizes for trust. Consumer optimizes for flow.

B2B

Enterprise / Business

Named agent instances for internal teams and multi-tenant platforms.

  • Strict tenant and department boundaries
  • Entra ID, RBAC, Conditional Access
  • Auditability, private networking, data residency
  • Predictable capacity and SLA
Security - Governance - Reliability
B2C

Consumer / End-user

High-volume, often short-lived sessions for users, teams, and playgrounds.

  • Fast activation and simple operations
  • User-level sandbox isolation
  • External identity or API-driven access
  • Aggressive scale-to-zero and pay-per-use
Cost - Speed - Simplicity

Key Technical Considerations - Stateful design

Persist first. Then hibernate safely.

1

Start

Resolve the agent ID and load its JSON state from Blob.

2

Act

Handle messages, tools, and governed model calls.

3

Save

Write conversation and tool state after every change.

4

Idle

Evict, pause, or scale to zero without a final flush.

5

Resume

Restore directly from the latest durable Blob version.

Single source of truth: <AGENT_ID>.json in Blob Cool tier with versioning. No separate Redis hot cache.
